Stemma privacy policy

Effective 12 October 2026

Stemma is an Android app for reading Wiktionary, the free dictionary. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: the app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and the developer does not receive any information about you.

Information sent over the internet

To show dictionary content, the app requests it directly from servers run by the Wikimedia Foundation (en.wiktionary.org and upload.wikimedia.org). These requests are made when you search, open an entry, play a pronunciation, or view the Explore screen. Each request contains the word or search text involved, the app's name and version, and, as with every internet request, your IP address. The Wikimedia Foundation handles this information under its own privacy policy. The developer of Stemma does not operate any server and does not receive these requests.

Links to other websites open in your browser and are covered by those websites' policies.

Information stored on your device

Saved words, offline copies of saved entries, reading history, recent searches and settings are stored on your device, and the developer never receives them. You can clear reading history and recent searches in the app's Settings, remove saved words from the Saved tab, and remove everything by uninstalling the app. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include saved words, history and settings in that backup.

Children

Stemma is a general-audience reference app and is not directed at children under 13.
