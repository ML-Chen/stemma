English fabada

    Borrowed from Asturian fabada, from faba (“bean”) + -ada (suffix denoting a meal made from an ingredient). Faba is derived from Latin faba (“bean”),[1] possibly ultimately from Proto-Indo-European *bʰab-, *bʰabʰ- (“fava bean”), from a European substrate.

    Pronunciation

    Noun

    fabada (countable and uncountable, plural fabadas)

    1. (uncountable) A stew from Asturias, Spain, made of white beans, pork (including chorizo, lacón (cured pork shoulder), and morcilla (blood sausage)), and other ingredients; (countable) a particular variety of this stew.
      Synonym: fabada asturiana
